Ads in MindPin

Last updated: May 1, 2025

MindPin displays banner ads to support free use of the app. These ads are served through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).

We do not use personalized ads. MindPin does not collect or store user data, and the app does not use tracking for ad targeting. You may occasionally see contextual or general ads, but no personal data is shared or used.

Why are there ads?

We use ads to support the development and maintenance of MindPin while keeping the app 100% free and offline-first.

Ad Preferences

You can manage your ad preferences for Google services by visiting My Ad Center.

If you upgrade to a future premium version of MindPin, ads may be removed.
